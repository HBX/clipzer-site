Clipzer Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Changsha Imaginary Technology Co., Ltd handles information in Clipzer. Clipzer is a local-first macOS menu bar app for clipboard history, quick notes, image compression, and utility tools.

1. Local Processing

Clipzer stores clipboard history, quick notes, settings, keyboard shortcut settings, excluded app lists, and temporary tool data on your Mac. Image compression and other tool processing are completed locally on your device.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, upload, or remotely store clipboard content.
  • We do not collect, upload, or remotely store quick notes.
  • We do not upload images, tool inputs, or tool outputs.
  • We do not create accounts or provide cloud sync.
  • We do not include advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or third-party tracking.

3. Clipboard, Notes, Images, and Tool Data

Content you copy, notes you save, images you choose to process, and content you enter into or generate with tools are used only to complete actions you initiate on your device. Clipzer does not send this data to us or to third-party servers.

4. System Permissions

Clipzer may request macOS Accessibility permission for shortcuts you trigger, reading or replacing selected text, and pasting content back into the target app. This permission is used only for local features, not for data collection or upload.

5. Mac App Store

Clipzer is distributed and purchased through the Mac App Store. Purchase, refund, billing, and store-related data are handled by Apple under its own policies. We do not collect this data from Clipzer.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete individual clipboard records, clear history, delete quick notes inside Clipzer, or remove the app and related local app data through macOS. After local data is deleted, we cannot restore it for you.

7. Updates to This Policy

If Clipzer's data handling changes, we will update this Privacy Policy and publish the new effective date on this page.

8. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us at 3284591423@qq.com.
