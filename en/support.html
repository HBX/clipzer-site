FAQ

Why do some features need Accessibility permission?

Accessibility permission is used for global shortcuts you trigger, reading or replacing selected text, and pasting content back into the target app. It is not used to upload data.

Does Clipzer upload clipboard, note, image, or tool content?

No. Clipboard history, quick notes, image compression, tool inputs, and tool outputs are processed locally. Clipzer does not collect or upload this content.

Does Clipzer have accounts, cloud sync, ads, or analytics?

No. Clipzer does not create accounts, provide cloud sync, or include advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or third-party tracking.

How do I delete local records?

You can delete individual clipboard records, clear history, delete quick notes inside Clipzer, or remove the app and related local app data through macOS.
